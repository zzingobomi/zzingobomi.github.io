Privacy policy

This policy covers the Google OAuth app "rclone" registered by the owner of this page.

  • The app is used only by its owner, with rclone, to copy the owner's own files between the owner's computers and the owner's Google Drive.
  • It is not offered to anyone else. It does not collect, store or share anyone else's data.
  • Files read from or written to Google Drive stay on the owner's computers and in the owner's Google Drive. Nothing is sent to any other service.
  • Access can be removed at any time in the Google Account's third-party access settings.
